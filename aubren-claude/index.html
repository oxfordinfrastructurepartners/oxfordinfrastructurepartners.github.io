01 The opportunity

Turning what Aubren already does into a steady sales pipeline

Aubren works in mission-critical air management. It retrofits existing sites, supplies intelligent controls, and offers the Akrivis range of precision cooling and air-management equipment. The commercial opportunity is to turn that work into a steady pipeline across data centres and telecom estates.

Oxford Infrastructure Partners can support Aubrey Nuzum, Ray O’Connor, and the wider commercial team by adding focused market-development capacity around repeatable applications, not bespoke projects.

03 Where we would focus

Data centres

  • Existing facilities with ageing, fixed-speed, or inefficient cooling infrastructure.

  • Operators seeking energy reductions without replacing entire cooling systems.

  • Fibre, modular, and edge environments requiring compact, scalable cooling.

  • Estates where a monitored retrofit trial could establish a business case for wider deployment.

  • Prospects under pressure to improve resilience, energy performance, carbon outcomes, or power availability.

04 Where we would focus

Telecom infrastructure

  • Tower operators and mobile networks managing large distributed estates.

  • Sites where conventional cooling creates excessive energy or maintenance costs.

  • Regions where heat, humidity, dust, or sandstorms complicate free-cooling deployment.

  • Operators willing to test a controlled solution at selected sites before a broader rollout.

  • Infrastructure owners seeking central monitoring, adaptive controls, and fewer compressor cycles.

06 Pricing, terms and ROI

We agree the scope first, then the price

Pricing and terms

Pricing and terms would be set once Aubren and Oxford Infrastructure Partners have agreed the target market, the number of accounts, the level of sales activity, the internal support available and what counts as a qualified opportunity. The agreement would then set out:

  • the services included;
  • the initial term and review points;
  • the responsibilities of each team;
  • the reporting measures;
  • how travel and other direct costs are handled;
  • any commercial terms linked to qualified opportunities or completed business.

How we would measure return

The review would compare the agreed cost of the work with the qualified pipeline and completed business it helps to produce, measured by:

  • target accounts researched;
  • relevant stakeholders reached;
  • qualified meetings held;
  • RFQs or supplier-onboarding processes entered;
  • pilot opportunities created;
  • revenue won.

No price, return or revenue forecast will be given until Aubren and Oxford Infrastructure Partners have agreed the commercial assumptions.
